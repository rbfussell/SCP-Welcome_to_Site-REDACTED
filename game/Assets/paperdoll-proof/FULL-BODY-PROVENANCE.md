## Registration limits

The generator did not provide production-ready matching silhouettes or perfect atlas alignment. The version-2 manifest authors source regions and front/back destinations. Paired socks/shoes have separate visual parts under one item ID. Long sleeves suppress stand-in base-arm edges through explicitly authored visual cutouts; this does not alter inventory fitting or access. Native render/picking tests and visual review qualify this particular reference body, not arbitrary anatomy, cloth deformation or world animations. Low-alpha colored fringe is ignored by the shared fixture cutoff of 0.1; source pixels are preserved.
