Real jacket badge clip

Implemented bounded reference-art fixture, November 6: the field coat now offers a real exterior badge clip. The painted white card/red clip has been removed from a new preserved-alpha art version. Only an actual attached badge appears in the worn/enlarged jacket views. See the implementation guide, exact art prompt/provenance and recorded checks. This is equipment design, not an SCP canon claim.

Physical equipment

The coat's front badge-clip support accepts the existing badge at (0,2), with exposed-access checks and a 100 g limit. It is distinct from the jacket's four real pockets and has no storage or lid. Shirt and jacket share the same badge interface and physical item; paid transfer never duplicates it.

Open or zipped jackets retain the badge. A front-worn backpack covers it, blocking manipulation and reader entry while preserving attachment. Loaded jacket removal carries the badge once. A jacket held in hand does not count as wearing or directly holding the badge. The existing credential service handles these states without a coat-specific exception.

Hybrid gear interaction

The enlarged gear surface now preserves pocket/zipper menus and direct storage alongside mounts. Compatible attachments near a support use its mount quote; ordinary items on exposed pocket material use its normal compartment packing quote. An invalid explicit mount cannot become a pocket drop. An opaque attached badge blocks drops into the hidden pocket behind it, while exposed pocket material remains usable.

Mounted contour highlighting uses the same projection as drawing and picking. Source-grid placement, transfers, ground staging, body inspection, mount clearance, coverage and time/AP remain shared inventory facts.

Artwork and continuation

The coat bundle contains a new byte-identical copy of built-in imagegen output. Original atlas/source art is preserved; only coat frames/crops use the new version. Asset authoring records bundle/provenance conventions. Full-body presentation renders the separate badge in open/closed front states; appearance still distinguishes this reference proof from production world clothing. Worn-only shoe masks are unrelated to the badge-free edit.

Placement is slightly inward over the left pocket to clear the default back-pack harness. Arbitrary body/coat poses, world wardrobe composition and animated gear closures remain future work. The enlarged coat still uses its closed reference image with authoritative state menus.

Kit remains 20 definitions/40 instances; no extra mutable region or save field is added. Immutable support identity changes, so start a fresh scene. Matching schema-15 continuation preserves paid transfers and attached children.
