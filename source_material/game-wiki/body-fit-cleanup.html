Fitting and layering

The geometry evaluator supports an optional authored straight-span range. The native belt extends its five-cell core to at most ten cells, independently at each end until the body boundary. Both end contacts and every intervening cell must fit. It cannot bridge a gap or exceed its limit. Ordinary shapes stay fixed. The author sheet separates storage dimensions from body-local fit.

At chest anchor (-4,8), rotations 60° and 120° form a real ten-cell-per-strap X. The latest base garment counts once; two straps fit, a third at the same clothed intersection fails. Worn-stack projection uses resolved cells for bands and physical interference on front/back surfaces. Access cover, restraint and contact exposure remain distinct.

The five central authored webbing points remain inside the guaranteed core. Extensions do not create new mounts or resize gear-view artwork. Hook/provider chains retain composed frames, shared clearance, load and ancestry checks.
