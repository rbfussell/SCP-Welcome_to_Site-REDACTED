OTF Beta-1 ("White Lotus") is to subconsciously influence key members of various meatspace telecommunication agencies towards phasing out landline and payphone services. [[[Oneiroi | Oneiroi West]]] will be provided covert aid in repairing damage caused by SCP-1498-Δ.



Corporeal containment of the anomaly is to remain under the jurisdiction of SCPF Site-77.



In the event that [[span class="hazard"]][ONEIROHAZARD EXPUNGED][[/span]] occurs within a Foundation Collective facility, attendant personnel must detonate its ergo-nuclear failsafe immediately.



Travel to and from SCP-1498-Ω is currently prohibited by order of the O5 Convocation.

SCP-1498 is the shared designation for --30-- 31 ambulatory sets of corporeal phones & cords that have each been assembled to look like sheep. Text has been printed on the underside of each instance's head: 



[[div class="blockquote"]]

[[=]]

**☎ DIAL-A-DREAM ☎**



BROUGHT TO YOU BY THE BELL TELEPHONE COLLECTIVE



YOUR PERFECT DREAM IS ONLY A PHONE AWAY!

[[/=]]

[[/div]]



SCP-1498 appears to host a collective consciousness (SCP-1498-Ω), which continually propagates a set of recurrent elements (SCP-1498-Δ) across the dreams of all corporeal landline users. All attempts to either contain SCP-1498-Ω or excise SCP-1498-Δ have thus far failed.



SCP-1498-Ω is primarily known as the Bell Telephone Collective. Within oneiric space, it is located in the western region of the Greater Oort Supercluster, twenty miliscreams north of [[[xiupania | Xiu Pan]]] and six leagues widdershins of [[[scp-4497 | Flavor Town]]]. From the outside, SCP-1498-Ω possesses the appearance of a chartreuse tesseract large enough to hold 2-3 Wisconsins. Its interior’s appearance is largely unknown, as individuals that enter SCP-1498-Ω are rarely given opportunity to depart.



[[include <a href="/component:image-block">component:image-block</a>

| name=spiraldial.png

| caption=SCP-1498-Δ in action.

| width=300px

| align=right]]



SCP-1498-Δ primarily manifests as a corded phone in an incongruous location (under the sink, inside an active volcano, orbiting Jupiter, etc.) that rings until it is physically interacted with. In the event that a dreamer picks up a 1498-Δ handset, they will be connected to an operator for a dream augmentation service. After discussing the various changes the subject would like to see done, the phone will play a series of tones that [[span class="hazard"]][ONEIROHAZARD EXPUNGED][[/span]], forcing the surrounding dream to restart with the requested alterations in place.



As callers cannot remember the original iteration of their dream after experiencing [[span class="hazard"]][ONEIROHAZARD EXPUNGED][[/span]], SCP-1498-Δ has been deemed a negligible risk to the Waking Veil. However, SCP-1498-Δ's extensive use of sound-based neural exploits[[footnote]]Colloquially known to Oneiroi as "mindphreaking."[[/footnote]] has caused significant structural and emotional damage to portions of Oneiroi West's infrastructure.



Other consistently observed SCP-1498-Δ elements include, but are not limited to:



* Ringing

* Rapid key movement

* Dead air

* The sensation of tangling

* Vertigo

* Long-distance fees

* "I want to see you."

**Addendum 1498.1**

Correlated Phenomena

[[/div]]



[[div class="modal"]]



**Date:** August 4th, 1922



**Sphere:** Oneiric



**Description:** Alexander Graham Bell's family experiences the same dream following his burial at Beinn Bhreagh.[[footnote]]**Beinn Bhreagh:** Bell's estate.[[/footnote]] Each reports peering through a window of Beinn Bhreagh Hall and seeing Bell converse with an unidentified figure. Upon entering the hall, both are gone; in their place, a [[[activity-documentation-file-11-a | large oak tree]]] festooned with coiled cords has grown from the floor. They are awoken by the sound of a phone ringing.



[[/div]]



[[div class="modal"]]



**Date:** August 5th, 1923



**Sphere:** Oneiric



**Description:** First known instance of oneirospheric disruption caused by SCP-1498-Δ-derived mindphreaking. Multiple telephone poles rapidly sprout from the floor of Bite Outta Crime, an Oneiroi West gastrotheque and recreational felony parlour. An officer of the [[[scp-4917 | OWPD]]] attempts to arrest the telephone poles under charges of general tresspassery, but is reminded that all illegal acts are permitted on the premises.



[[/div]]



[[div class="modal"]]



**Date:** February 1968



**Sphere:** Corporeal



**Description:**  Joybubbles[[footnote]]**Joybubbles:** The father of “phreaking” (phone hacking). Legally changed name to “Joybubbles” in 1991.[[/footnote]] receives fifteen calls of unknown origin over the course of a month, beginning shortly after his temporary suspension from the University of South Florida for selling hacked phone calls. Each call is composed of random voice fragments. The first is transcribed below:



[[div class="blockquote"]]



Am I coming through? Ozone taste in the doctor parade. Listen. Listen? Scoop the bassoon. Close the signal, over-stand?



//<Five seconds of room tone.>//



Come here.



[[/div]]



[[/div]]



[[div class="modal"]]



**Date:** April 7th, 1995



**Sphere:** Corporeal



**Description:** All wired telecommunications devices in the city of Mobile, Alabama experience a localized [[[last-light-hub | Babel-class incomprehension event]]] for three hours. Portable phones are unaffected.



[[/div]]



[[div class="blockquote custom"]]

**Addendum 1498.2**

OoI Interview Log

[[/div]]



[[div class="cloudbox" style="top: 3.5rem; margin-bottom: 4.5rem;"]]



**Foreword:** The following is an interview held between Agent Frond and an unidentified member of SCP-1498-Ω (designated Oneiroi of Interest 1498-03). Ocular footage was recorded and transmitted by the surveillance parasite implanted in Agent Frond's shadow.[[footnote]]**Shadow:** A corporeal dreamer's oneiric avatar.[[/footnote]]



  [[div style="border-top: .1rem solid var(--indianYellow); opacity: .5;"]]

  [[/div]]



= [[size x-large]]{{**✹ BEGIN RECALL ✹**}}[[/size]]



//<Agent Frond stands in a well-lit phone booth. It is night. She picks up the handset, slips two Oneirian Laguz into the currency slot, then enters █-███-███-████ into the keypad. The phone rings twice before picking up to dead air.>//





[[include <a href="/component:image-block">component:image-block</a>

| name=midnight-booth.jpg

| caption=Still from ocular footage.

| width=250px

| align=right]]



**Agent Frond:** ...Hello? Is this the Bell Telephone Collective? I was told this number was--



**OoI-1498-03:** Hello? Yes, this is the. //<Bell chime.>// Collective. To you! Am I speaking?



**Agent Frond:** My name is Agent Frond, I'm a member of a dream regulation organization. Do you have a moment to answer some questions about your communal consciousness?



**OoI-1498-03:** You? Can call me. About? Anything!



**Agent Frond:** Thank you for your cooperation. Firstly, I'd like to ask why your collective has continued to--



**OoI-1498-03:** [DATA EXPUNGED]



**Agent Frond:** I see. And do you have any intention of stopping?



**OoI-1498-03:** No? No. We will not! Cease. Operations? Our operators are. Toll-free! Twenty-four hours! In service!



**Agent Frond:** Can you tell me why you operate the way you do?



**OoI-1498-03:** Sound is. Strange. Voice is. Strange?



**Agent Frond:** Strange in what way?



**OoI-1498-03:** Speech is. Meaning? Inside of. Language! Inside of. Waves? 

Pieces are. Bound. To? Get lost! In transit. Miscommunication. Inevitable?



**Agent Frond:** I don't understand. Can you elaborate?



**OoI-1498-03:** Have you! Heard. Machines? Dream of. Electric! Sheep. But that's not. Correctamundo! No. No? It's the. Electric! Sound. That dreams? Of.



**Agent Frond:** ...Of what?



**OoI-1498-03:** [[span class="hazard"]][ONEIROHAZARD EXPUNGED][[/span]]



//<Surveillance parasite ceases transmission.>//



= [[size x-large]]{{**✹ END RECALL ✹**}}[[/size]]



  [[div style="border-bottom: .1rem solid var(--indianYellow); opacity: .5;"]]

  [[/div]]



**Postscript:** After her interview with OoI-1498-03, Agent Frond's corporeal body could not be roused from REM sleep, transforming into an SCP-1498 instance over the course of the following 72 hours. All attempts to communicate with Agent Frond have failed and her shadow has been declared lost.
