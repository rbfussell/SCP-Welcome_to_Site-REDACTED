Badge-controlled door access

Implemented bounded fixture, November 6: an exposed worn valid badge can open the test reader automatically during realtime approach. Otherwise it must be equipped directly in hand and presented with E. Stored or covered badges do not count. This is the user's selected game rule, not an SCP canon assertion. See the current guide and actual results.

Physical presentation

door-7-5-u requires issuer site-test, clearance 1. The existing separate personnel badge supplies that policy and starts in the spare backpack. Shirt clip mounting, body coverage, independent transfers and ground staging remain normal physical transactions. Painted badge pixels, open views and an accessible pocket grant no entry.

The credential service checks actual worn attachment ancestry and exposed occupancy, or direct actor-root hand placement. It validates issuer, tier, positive condition and optional assigned actor identity. Bearer mode is the provisional test default, pending the user's identity-binding choice; both modes are supported. The same service supports NPC queries without player authority overrides.

Real time and tactical time

A moving realtime wearer in physical reader reach can open it without stopping. Idle actors do not repeatedly reopen a just-closed door. E with a worn badge uses ordinary door timing: immediate in realtime, 2 AP/300 local ms in combat. E with a held badge buys 500 local ms/2 AP presentation through normal item handling; AP is spent only in combat. The item remains in hand until completion. Tactical input holds never grant free automatic opening.

Other walls/cabinets, paused/held local time, coverage, damaged/wrong credentials, inadequate tier and unpowered reader can refuse access. A closed reader reports denial through status. Once open, it is ordinary shared space; no reservation or per-person collision exemption is invented. Closing uses existing obstruction checks.

Continuation and scope

Schema-15 persistence saves the existing door set and paid Inventory work. Appended PresentBadge uses optional request TargetId; detached restore checks the reader and canonical costs. Completion rechecks access, hand/display state, policy, power, condition and interruption. Map/kit immutable identity changes; use a fresh scene.

This specific reader's authored power flag refuses opening when off. Working circuits and device-specific power-failure policies remain infrastructure design. No production credential issuance/revocation, expiration, reader orientation/lighting model, NPC reader planning, escort policy, SCiPnet or OSCP account authorization is implemented. Current world wardrobe sprites remain baked and do not determine credential eligibility.

Domain checks cover policy, presentation, NPC queries, AP/time, interruption and exact continuation. Native checks exercise real inventory drag/E/approach. The item catalog exposes the immutable credential field.
