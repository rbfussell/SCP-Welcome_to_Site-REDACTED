Every depicted pocket is usable

Current authoring contract, November 5: if artwork shows a pocket or pouch, it must have real usable storage. Earlier decorative-pocket exceptions are superseded. This is ordinary equipment design, not SCP canon. The implementation guide records IDs, provisional capacities, initial states and verification boundaries.

Current coverage

The field coat has four front pockets (chest, chest-left, lower-left, lower-right). Trousers have two snug/unsealable hip pockets, two buttoned cargo pockets and two buttoned rear pockets. Front/back cargo illustrations share the same real inventories. The backpack has main, side and front-pouch compartments on its bag side, with separate closures. The shirt retains two buttoned chest pockets and its distinct badge clip. Pocket contours link these illustrations to authoritative regions; pixels never create capacity automatically.

New pockets start secured on every kit instance. Coat enlarged art uses a metadata crop of its existing worn source so layouts agree; original PNG bytes are preserved. Enlarged views offer explicit compartment buttons where the current illustration lacks a hotspot, including rear trouser pockets. Asset authoring and the visual item catalog must expose the same region identities and registrations.

Physical authority

Inventory services retain capacity, packing, independent closures, paid time/AP, source retention and completion revalidation. Coverage uses the actual front/back mouth: a jacket may cover rear pockets even when its front is open. Transfers, ground staging, body inspection, worn container drops and sprite drops keep their normal access rules. Opening one view never duplicates or freely opens another inventory.

The kit remains 20 definitions/40 instances but immutable content identity changes. Use a fresh scene; matching schema-15 persistence adds no new mutable field. Shirt/badge design distinguishes physical storage/attachment from the bounded credential reader and future production security; painted badge pixels grant no permission.

Evidence and limits

Domain checks verify region sets, closed refusal, paid storage, rear mouths and exact loaded continuation. Native contour checks and pack checks exercise actual input. See actual results. Automatic image segmentation, arbitrary body variants, flap animation, final capacity balance and world wardrobe composition remain future work.
