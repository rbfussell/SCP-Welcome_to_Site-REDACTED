## Initial prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: transparent layered paperdoll sprite registration atlas for an SCP simulation. Reference image is ONLY the identity/style reference: same short dark-haired adult male, pale/olive skin, burgundy shirt and dark charcoal facility jacket. Create ONE 4-column by 2-row sprite sheet, equal cells, each cell using exactly the SAME upright upper-body pose, scale and registration. Top row FRONT, bottom row BACK. Column 1: bare upper-body man, head through hips, neutral black briefs at bottom, arms hanging slightly away from torso, hands visible. Column 2: ONLY his burgundy collared long-sleeved button shirt, worn shape fitted to that identical invisible body; no body, face, hands or trousers. Column 3: ONLY his dark charcoal facility jacket fully CLOSED, worn shape fitted to that same invisible body; no skin, face, hands, trousers, underlying shirt. Column 4: ONLY that exact jacket OPEN down the front with jacket panels to each side and a genuinely transparent gap showing where the shirt would be. Garments include sleeves, no floating mannequin, no skin. On the BACK the open/closed jacket may look identical. All eight panels share exact shoulder, neck, wrist and waist positions relative to their cell. This is a practical compositing sheet: no body baked into garment layers, no clothing baked into bare body. Detailed visibly pixelated high-color Fallout 2 inspired sprite art, realistic adult proportions, overhead-neutral studio lighting, orthographic straight front/back paperdoll camera rather than world isometric. Transparent background everywhere outside actual layers, including inside open jacket gap and shirt neck hole. No scenery, shadows on background, text, labels, borders, grid, checkerboard or extra items. Portrait upper-body crops only, not full body. Leave safe transparent padding within each panel; assets must not touch adjacent cells.

## Selected revision prompt

Use case: precise-object-edit. Fix this layered game sprite atlas registration and spacing. Preserve the same male identity, all eight subjects, burgundy shirt, charcoal jacket, detailed pixel sprite style. Produce a generous transparent canvas, four columns and two rows. CRITICAL: SHRINK EACH SUBJECT to about 70 percent of its current cell size, preserving aspect ratio. Each subject must fit entirely within its own cell with WIDE EMPTY TRANSPARENT gutters both vertically and horizontally, so top-row hands never overlap the bottom-row head. This is a sprite atlas used by cutting separate rectangles; no piece of any neighboring subject may be within a cell. Align EVERY torso, shoulder, neck, wrist and waist to the SAME positions relative to their cells: row1 FRONT bare body / shirt-only / closed-jacket-only / open-jacket-only; row2 BACK of those four. Garments must fit the reference body's pose, without skin/hands/head baked into them. Base body includes head to hips with arms and hands. No red edge fringe, no bright-colored halo, no colored strokes, no background pixel dust, no cast shadow. Source transparency must be clean and actual alpha zero outside subjects and within neck holes and open jacket gap. Preserve the truly open jacket front as two separated halves joined across shoulders, no shirt in that gap. Keep same detail and colors, no labels or text or grid or checkerboard. The spacing and identical local registration are more important than filling the canvas.

## Qualification notes

The generator did not supply exact equal cells or production-ready alignment. Source rectangles are manually registered, with a head/neck foreground part to put the rear collar behind the face. Very low alpha edge fringe remains in the untouched source; the fixture discards alpha ≤ 0.1 in both rendering and picking. Garments and body are stand-ins requiring further art qualification before production coverage. This source image is 1774 × 887 RGBA.
