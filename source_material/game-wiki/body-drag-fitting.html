Direct body-fit drops

Implemented native interaction, October 30: drag one visible stored clothing item onto the diagnostic body to preview and purchase its exact fit. See the runtime guide, inventory, presentation and implementation ledger. This is ordinary equipment handling, not an SCP canon adaptation.

Exact placement, normal work

The drop surface shares click/drag polygon picking and symmetric body geometry. The pointer hex is the authored anchor. Empty margins/gaps refuse placement. Mask and contacts appear at the exact proposed pose; failed drops do not reposition, rotate, repack or remove anything.

R over the body uses six orientations. R over storage uses two, even when body mode is visible; provider views retain six attachment turns. Independent transient drafts leave stored state unchanged. Esc cancels the drag and hides the interface without closure changes. Paused release cannot perform free dressing.

Access and continuation

InventoryBodyFit offers PreviewBodyFitDrop for one currently inspectable item. Closed/inaccessible ancestors, foreign equipment and non-wearables expose no geometry. The selector still lists own worn/held clothing only, without scanning hidden storage.

Release submits ordinary Move with its exact pose. Geometry, overlay bands, coverage, restraints, outermost packs, provider exposure/clearance and access remain authoritative. Source-dependent time/AP is paid before completion; the source stays put until then. Commitment revalidates the graph. Front-mounted refits preserve their mount; stored clothing uses its authored default.

Persistence was schema 14 at this slice’s qualification with unchanged immutable content. UI drafts are transient; accepted paid requests continue through existing saves. Saves matching the preceding body-cleanup content remain supported.
