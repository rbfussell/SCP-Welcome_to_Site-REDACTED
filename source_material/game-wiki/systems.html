Systems directory

  • World and geometry — Owns facility geometry, doors, lighting, physical occupancy, shared state and dynamic blockers. Square construction geometry is distinct from hex actor positions.
  • Actors and navigation — Owns actor state, independent facing, hex routes, movement progress, reservations, rerouting, doors and bounded patrol.
  • Credential readers and physical presentation — Validates reader policy against real displayed or hand-equipped credentials, physical reach and condition.
  • Perception and awareness — Computes detection and qualified observation using facing, attention, blink, lighting, geometry and observer kind. PlayerAwareness tracks acquired contacts and interruption preferences.
  • Anomaly host and SCP modules — Owns native module registration, evaluation, state binding, scoped world queries and validated effects. SCP-650 owns its own rule, target/pose selection, RNG, retry and witness state.
  • Personnel orders and recovery — Owns authored duties, orders, bounded communications, received reports, trolley loading/pushing/unloading and receiving-footprint compliance.
  • Employment and relief — Owns scheduled appointments, physical observation posts, relief, bounded overtime, delivered staffing reports and saved continuation.
  • Physical inventory, clothing and body fit — Owns one physical item graph, map-local floor piles and top-only access, nested containers, rectangular packing, physical closures, ancestor access, paid manipulation, symmetric body-local hex fit, explicit stored-clothing fit drops and bounded strap spans, pocket masks, actual worn order, overlay bands, nested mount providers and shared exterior clearance.
  • Tactical turns and training — Owns local turn/AP scheduling, training equipment and points, pending actions, acquired contacts and autonomous training-opponent decisions.
  • OSCP and communications — Owns native applications, workstation/handset service access, acquired records and a bounded regional-intake fixture.
  • Player feedback and journal — Owns bounded acquired player messages, visibility episodes, witness/discovery presentation events and delivery feedback.
  • Diagnostics and causal trace — Owns a bounded causal trace with world/local timestamps, subjects, causes and reasons.
  • Persistence and continuation — Owns outer versioned snapshot validation and detached candidate restore. Domain codecs preserve their own state and pending work.
  • Godot presentation and runtime — Owns scene lifecycle, input bindings, simulation tick adaptation, sprites, projection, wall cutaways, HUD, inventory windows, OSCP and smoke drivers.
  • Simulation command facade — Coordinates validated commands, shared queries, explicit time controls, tick ordering and snapshot entry points.
