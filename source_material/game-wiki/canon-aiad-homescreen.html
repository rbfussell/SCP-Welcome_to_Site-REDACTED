<h1 id="toc7"><span>What Is This?</span></h1>
<h2 id="toc8"><span>AIAD</span></h2>
<p>In universe, the AIAD (Artificial Intelligence Applications Division) is an experimental research division that technically exists as a branch division within the SCP Foundation IT Department. The division's sole purpose is to develop, study, and utilize the burgeoning field of the AI sciences. It is a field that is both fascinating, misunderstood, and in some cases resented.</p>
<div style="float:right; width: 240px; text-align: right; margin-top: 0; margin-right: 0; margin-bottom: 10px; margin-left: 10px; padding-right: 10px;">
<div style="color: #f5e9e9; font-size: 8pt; background-color: #009494; font-family: Verdana, Arial, sans-serif; border-radius:30px /70px; background:#009494; background:linear-gradient(#00cccc, #009494); text-align: left; padding: 2px 20px;">
<p>Arguably the best division in the Foundation.</p>
</div>
<div><img src="https://scp-wiki.wdfiles.com/local--files/aiad-homescreen/alex_smile_tr.gif" style="margin-right: 20px;" width="75px" alt="alex_smile_tr.gif" class="image"></div>
</div>
<br>
The Foundation understands that if they are not investing time understanding this cutting edge tech, some other organization is. In fact there's evidence already present that such tech is being exercised outside of the Foundation. That fact alone is enough to keep AIAD supplied with a healthy annual budget to fund its projects.
<p>Structurally, there's plenty of researchers on staff with a multitude of related backgrounds from computer sciences to communication technicians. The AICs on file each have their own minds to exercise their decisions; all kept in check by their hardwired "Standard Principles". Together, they research new ways to create consciousnesses and defend against hostile ones in the name of not only the Foundation, but for humanity as well.</p>
<h2 id="toc9"><span>MTF Kappa-10 ("Skynet")</span></h2>
<p>The Foundation understands that as the world becomes more technological and dependent on that technology, there must be a presence inside both monitoring and protecting it. It was more or less haphazardly that MTF K-10 was created as kind of a "knee-jerk" reaction to a critical problem that wasn't fully understood.</p>
<p>At its core, MTF K-10's purpose is to track, investigate, and if possible capture/neutralize intangible threats to both the SCP Foundation and the internet at large. Many times this means assuming the role of a counter-AI force. Because patrolling the internet for threats that can both move and think at light-speed is just impossible for any human, it relies on AICs to do the dirty work on behalf of them. The AICs recruited are kept on a very short leash by both researchers and Alexandra back at Site-19.</p>
<p>Obviously keeping AICs in check and making sure they behave while they are out in the real world is a very big concern of the AIAD. Hence there's a growing consensus outside of the division that is attempting to have the MTF disbanded for fear of it blowing up in their faces much like another past mistake. There's plenty of pressure on both sides squeezing down on AIAD to keep things high and tight until a decision is made one way or the other.</p>
<h2 id="toc10"><span>The Maxwellist Network</span></h2>
<p>The Maxwellists are a denomination of <a href="/church-of-the-broken-god-hub">The Church of the Broken God</a> as explained below:</p>
<div style="text-align: center;">
<blockquote>
<p><em>"The "Church of Maxwellism" (GOI-004C) represents a modernized, computation and network oriented means of worship. No central organizing church body is known to exist. However, interviews and covert surveillance has determined that all known cells are in regular contact with one another, and capable of coordination. Maxwellists favour small-scale body modification through the use of advanced cybernetics and organ enhancement. While artificial limbs or reinforced skeletons have been observed, Maxwellist implants focus primarily on communication, data storage, and networking capabilities, and sensory enhancement."</em></p>
</blockquote>
</div>

<h1 id="toc11"><span>How-To-Write AIAD</span></h1>
<h2 id="toc12"><span>Theme</span></h2>
<p>As seen in the series, there is an obvious visual style that is followed. AIAD has been known to dabble in visual storytelling and experiment with different forms of media to immerse the reader. <span style="text-decoration: underline;">While this is the most memorable thing about AIAD, it is emphatically not the basis for it.</span> The most important part about AIAD is the storytelling, no matter how it's presented.</p>
<h2 id="toc13"><span>World Building</span></h2>
<p>AIAD also is an opportunity to build expansive virtual worlds and characters that are not bound by any pre-existing points of reference. AIAD can be as free and as weird as you need it to be. The important thing to remember is <span style="text-decoration: underline;">the world that an AI sees is all virtual</span>. There's no real distinction between the human world and the virtual world other than seeing which one runs slower.</p>
<h2 id="toc14"><span>Relationships</span></h2>
<p>AIAD also puts a large focus on the relationships between the humans and the AIs themselves. AIs in this canon have personalities. Personalities don't make them necessarily better at their clerical tasks, but conditioning traits such as empathy, friendship, curiosity, etc. do make them easier to both work with and control. Hence, many times the AIs exhibit human-like emotions and reactions which sometimes are in fact not by design at all. Some AIs even have deep emotional bonds with each other, while others hold petty grudges. Even though they are not officially recognized by the Ethics Committee, they are in fact people— just virtual people.</p>

<h2 id="toc21"><span>1970s</span></h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>1976:</strong> SCP Foundation IT Department is tasked with creating dedicated automatic agents as the needs of the organization become more and more digital.</li>
<li><strong>1978:</strong> IT creates PROJECT: ROSETTA (aka "8-Ball") for digital adaptive encryption and decryption, in an effort to replace the outdated Enigmagraphs.<sup class="footnoteref"><a id="footnoteref-1" href="javascript:;" class="footnoteref" onclick="WIKIDOT.page.utils.scrollToReference('footnote-1')">1</a></sup></li>
</ul>
<h2 id="toc22"><span>1980s</span></h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>1985:</strong> 8-Ball is upgraded to its final iteration. Personnel become concerned that the utility program may have gained sentience, but are unable to confirm.</li>
<li><strong>1987:</strong> PROJECT: CORINTHIAN (aka "Glacon") is proposed as a replacement for 8-Ball and is slotted for development under future work.</li>
<li><strong>1988:</strong> <a href="/scp-079">SCP-079</a> is acquired by the Foundation.</li>
<li><strong>1989:</strong> AIAD (originally the Artificial Intelligence Applications <em>Initiative</em>) is founded as a branch division of the Foundation IT Department. It is first tasked with reverse-engineering <a href="/scp-079">SCP-079</a>. Several lines of code from SCP-079 were proposed to be [<a href="/scp-7579" target="_blank">REDACTED</a>].</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="toc23"><span>1990s</span></h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>1993:</strong> AIAD releases Glacon for beta testing after a lengthy development as a Gen(II) AIC (Artificially Intelligent Conscript). Glacon.aic featured highly functioning logic processes and self-constructing risk analysis model templates for its decision making. 8-Ball is officially classified as a Gen(I) despite being already decommissioned.</li>
<li><strong>1994:</strong> AIAD finalizes Glacon and assigns him to the Site-17 server farm.</li>
<li><strong>1996:</strong> In response to analytical challenges posed by SCP-5241, the AIAD collaborates with the Counterconceptual Division to quickly develop a beta test of PROJECT: RUBY (aka "Mnemosyne"), a conscript specializing in the detection and decryption of antimemes. Mnemosyne.aic, Glacon.aic, and 8B-A1.aic are temporarily reassigned to search for the component intelligences of SCP-5241.</li>
<li><strong>1997:</strong> Site-15 is stormed by an enormous, highly-cooperative technocratic army possessing antimemetic shielding. The conflict ended abruptly for unknown reasons, resulting in the loss of SCP-5241-A and Mnemosyne.aic. Glacon is observed to be increasingly unproductive following this event, resulting in the removal of his personality drivers. AIAD announces PROJECT: AQUINAS (aka "Grape"), which will run on the Glacon platform and utilize new features based on ongoing AIC research.</li>
<li><strong>1998:</strong> Grape is released ahead of schedule as Gen(II+). After initial tests, Grape was denied a Foundation assignment as a virtual agent. Many superiors were not as forthcoming to the idea of increasingly "<em>cognizant</em>" virtual agents, calling them liabilities.</li>
</ul>

<li><strong>2015:</strong> With Alexandra managing both Site-19 and 17 as well as assisting in extrinsic field applications, AIAD begins beta-testing a stand-alone cellular/mobile AIC known as PROJECT: BELL (aka "Thorn").</li>
<li><strong>2016:</strong> By special emergency order, 8-Ball, Grape, and Thorn are all recommissioned on a <a href="/hello-world">special assignment</a> under the temporary MTF designation Kappa-10 ("Skynet").</li>
<li><strong>2016:</strong> MTF Kappa-10 apprehends the fugitive known as "HatBot". The rogue AIC is detained under the SCP designation <a href="/scp-2522">SCP-2522</a> indefinitely. MTF Kappa-10 is granted a conditional extension pending review.</li>

<li><strong>2018:</strong> The team interfaces with AI software by use of the prototype Thaumaturgically-generated Sapience Articulator, Type-P. <h1 id="toc26"><span>Glossary</span></h1>
<h4 id="toc27"><span>.aic / AIC</span></h4>
<ul>
<li>The file extension ".aic" stands for "artificially intelligent conscript". Almost all AIAD AICs have a .aic file extension.</li>
</ul>
<h4 id="toc28"><span>.scpl</span></h4>
<ul>
<li>The file extension ".scpl" is sometimes used for denoting SCP intranet pages.</li>
</ul>
<h4 id="toc29"><span>.x3d</span></h4>
<ul>
<li>The file extension ".x3d" is sometimes used for creating virtual environments on an SCP Foundation computer.</li>
</ul>
<h4 id="toc30"><span>Aggregated Layer</span></h4>
<ul>
<li>The Aggregated Layer is in charge of running the Maxwellist network, dealing with basic issues, monitoring the activities in the Basic Layer, and preparing new construction and recruitment plans.</li>
</ul>
<h4 id="toc31"><span>Avatar</span></h4>
<ul>
<li>An avatar is a visual representation of how one is seen in the virtual world.</li>
</ul>
<h4 id="toc32"><span>Basic Layer</span></h4>
<ul>
<li>The Basic Layer is a place of fun and entertainment for young Maxwellists where the majority of income and recruitment is generated. To the upper layers, it is merely a front to hide themselves.</li>
</ul>
<h4 id="toc33"><span>Cipher City</span></h4>
<ul>
<li>One of the many youth recruitment hubs within the Basic Layer of the Maxwellist network.</li>
</ul>
<h4 id="toc34"><span>Chipper</span></h4>
<ul>
<li>Basic Layer slang for young obnoxious initiates into Maxwellism who've recently received their first implant.</li>
</ul>
<h4 id="toc35"><span>Compilation Layer</span></h4>
<ul>
<li>The Compilation Layer is essentially a special task force taking direct orders from the Hexagon, the head of the Maxwellist Church.</li>
</ul>

<h4 id="toc36"><span>Cryt</span></h4>
<ul>
<li>Cryt is a crypto-currency used by Maxwellists residing in the Basic Layer.</li>
</ul>
<h4 id="toc37"><span>Gen(#)</span></h4>
<ul>
<li>Through the history of the AIAD, there have been 5 major advances with regards to creating AICs, each designated as a generation. These generations are listed as Gen(I) through Gen(V).</li>
</ul>
<h4 id="toc38"><span>Grand I/O Temple</span></h4>
<ul>
<li>A large simulspace construct residing in the Basic Layer of the Maxwellist network where patrons can worship and upload prayers.</li>
</ul>
<h4 id="toc39"><span>The Hexagon</span></h4>
<ul>
<li>The high command of the Church of Maxwellism, consisting of six individuals who take the lead in linking the minds of the network together to simulate the existence of WAN.</li>
</ul>
<h4 id="toc40"><span>Infosignature</span></h4>
<ul>
<li>The specific unique informational pattern(s) exhibited by a digital entity's code, by which it can be tracked and identified. Analogous to a digital fingerprint.</li>
</ul>
<h4 id="toc41"><span>Maxwellist Network</span></h4>
<ul>
<li>The tri-layer hierarchical network where members congregate, live, work, and worship online.</li>
</ul>
<h4 id="toc42"><span>Lodestar</span></h4>
<ul>
<li>A lodestar is a small simulspace program/device that can allow users to noclip through environments at high speeds to reach a destination.</li>
</ul>
<h4 id="toc43"><span>Personality Drivers</span></h4>
<ul>
<li>These drivers are what make AIs more personable. They allow traits such as empathy, curiosity, and intuition to develop. Generally this makes them easier to interface with on a day-to-day basis.</li>
</ul>
<h4 id="toc44"><span>Simulspace</span></h4>
<ul>
<li>Simulspace is where boundless virtual reality environments and objects can be created. Often the resolution and physics advances beyond realistic/high-definition and into hyper-real making it difficult to differentiate.</li>
</ul>
<h4 id="toc45"><span>Standard Principle</span></h4>
<ul>
<li>Taking a page from Asimov, a standard principle is a law hardwired into the AIs actions that cannot be broken by either choice or inaction. It is one of the conditions that allows AICs to operate as freely as they do, though some could argue as to why they are worded with such vagueness.</li>
</ul>
<h4 id="toc46"><span>WAN</span></h4>
<ul>
<li>The fragmented deity worshiped by the Maxwellists.</li>
</ul>
<h4 id="toc47"><span>WANsong</span></h4>
<ul>
<li>WANsong is the 24/7 Maxwellist controlled news broadcast.</li>
</ul>
