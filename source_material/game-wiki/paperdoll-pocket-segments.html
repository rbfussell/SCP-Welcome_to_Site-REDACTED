Paperdoll pocket segments

Implemented bounded reference-body interaction, November 5. I → Sprites now highlights and operates the jacket's existing front chest pocket directly, using its visible contour in open/closed jacket artwork. The viewer-left pocket maps to avatar-coat / chest; other illustrated pockets remain decorative. See the runtime/metadata guide, worn container drops, sprite equipment and appearance contract. This is ordinary equipment, not SCP canon.

Segments and authority

Optional PaperdollPart.pockets entries name a real region, side and normalized source-local polygon. Whole-part bag registrations remain separate. Pocket drawing/validation uses the current texture/alpha and normal layer order. Storage picking requires topmost visible material before a segment can target its compartment. Transparent gaps and covered pixels do not turn into clickable rectangles. A front-mounted pack hides the covered chest pocket.

These are bounded authored visual registrations, not new physical pocket masks or automatic segmentation. Inventory continues to own coverage, capacity, closures, access, time/AP and exact placement. Native definitions, kit identity and schema-15 continuation are unchanged; source PNGs are retained.

Interaction

Hover highlights just the segment and reads actual pocket state. Double-click inspects the jacket and opens that specific pocket through normal paid handling when necessary. Paused opening refuses. Right-click reads the pocket's Button/Unbutton action and securing preference, rather than the jacket-front zipper. The pocket remains a compartment of the jacket; dragging moves the owning garment through ordinary removal rules.

Drops reuse worn container packing, without free opening or fallback to body fitting. Zipping the jacket front does not button its chest pocket. Independent source views, body inspection and view-closing rules keep their existing behavior. Animation is not yet added.

Native checks cover source-alpha hover, paid double-click/exact restore, context fastening, direct storage, both jacket states, rear exclusion and pack occlusion. Focused --pocket-smoke and the larger inventory suites run the fixture. See actual evidence; building the wiki does not run gameplay tests.

Only this front chest segment is qualified. Other clothing pockets, additional pack segments, arbitrary bodies, automatic tracing, flap animation and world wardrobe composition remain future work. Asset organization and the item catalog expose registration evidence without becoming gameplay authority.
