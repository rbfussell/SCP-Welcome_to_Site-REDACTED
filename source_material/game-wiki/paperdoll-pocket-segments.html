Jacket interaction

Hover highlights just the segment and reads actual pocket state. Double-click inspects the jacket and opens that specific pocket through normal paid handling when necessary. Paused opening refuses. Right-click reads the pocket's Button/Unbutton action and securing preference, rather than the jacket-front zipper. The pocket remains a compartment of the jacket; dragging moves the owning garment through ordinary removal rules.

Drops reuse worn container packing, without free opening or fallback to body fitting. Zipping the jacket front does not button its chest pocket. Independent source views, body inspection and view-closing rules keep their existing behavior. Animation is not yet added.

Native checks cover source-alpha hover, paid double-click/exact restore, context fastening, direct storage, both jacket states, rear exclusion and pack occlusion.
