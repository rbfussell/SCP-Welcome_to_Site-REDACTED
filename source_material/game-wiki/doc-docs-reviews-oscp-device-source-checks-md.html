Research method

Read the local archive guide, resolve SCP-5810 and SCP-245 through the items index and inspect their wikitext. Search available item, tale, GOI and hub bodies for Foundation-issued phones, secure/encrypted handhelds and nearby SCiPNET/OSCP mentions, parsing one shard at a time. The scan returned 95 candidate pattern matches, including unrelated email/phone-word overlaps; matches were read in context rather than counted as corroborating facts. The inspected archive revision remains 278a69a68e4b85ac7135d061b9fbd6f1027e1a96.

Verify the live SCP-5810, SCP-5098, SCP-5457 and That's the Spirit! pages against the relevant local passages. Other candidates included explicit SCiPNET-phone mentions and phone queries in tales; these were unnecessary to establish the bounded precedents above. Absence of a standardized handset in this research is not proof that none exists anywhere on the wiki.

Confirmed design and remaining questions

Full OSCP opens from fixed, physically accessible workstations at facilities. The interface visibly includes the CRT monitor body framing its active screen; a flat window with a scanline shader alone does not meet that presentation target. Its software view supplies the strategic construction, mission planning, intelligence and equipment/material/network-management workspace.

The second profile is a carried secure Foundation phone for local-area mapping, current mission information and communications over permitted local/SCiPNET routes. A phone does not grant full workstation features merely because the actor has senior clearance. The user confirms limited mission/team requests, including backup and assigned-team commands, as well as records and communications. Both device profiles should support additional registered applications later. Exact navigation remains open.

Both profiles use shared records, acquired knowledge, validated services and normal device/session/time rules. Standalone OSCP launch uses an explicit test session/device context; combined play requires the real accessible device. Neither profile adds automatic pause or makes unobserved personnel and anomalies visible on a map. See the terminal contract and independent-mode decision.

The phone/inventory screen is planned as a shared character menu, with inventory on the right and a phone companion pane. Exact arrangement is deferred. The user confirmed on October 25 that opening it automatically pauses single-player; the fixed workstation remains real-time. These layout/application decisions do not claim source-defined OSCP phone features.

October 25 runtime follow-up: a bounded CRT/phone shell now implements these device profiles and native app registration. The underlying source/adaptation distinctions above still apply.
