<h1><span><span style="font-size:125%;">Welcome to the International Branches Hub</span></span></h1>
<h3><span>The contents of this page are currently [CLASSIFIED].</span></h3>
<p><strong>Personnel are reminded that while this hub is classified as Security Clearance Level 1, files within this hub are subject to various classifications, and verified credentials will be necessary to access those files.</strong></p>
</div>
<hr>
<p>The International Branches Hub lists articles from other branches of the SCP Foundation that have been translated into English. Most of these translations were originally posted on the <a href="https://scp-int.wikidot.com/scp-international">International Translation Archive</a> of the SCP-Foundation (SCP-INT), a site where members of various branches post translations in English to make them available to an international audience. The pages listed on this hub are only a small selection of the content available on the International Translation Archive, and we encourage you to go there to check out a much larger number of translated articles.</p>
<p>It's worth noting that branches are named for their language, not their nationality. While you may be reading this on the "English branch" (SCP-EN), that doesn't mean everyone here is from England, and the same is true for the other branches!</p>
<p>When reading, rating and commenting these pages, keep in mind that some branches have their own background for the Foundation, which may be different to what you're used to. Feedback, questions and corrections to spelling and grammar are just as welcome here as they are on the rest of the site.</p>
<p>If you want to translate articles written in English into another language, you'll need to become a member of that branch's wiki - a list of officially recognised branches with links to their own wikis can be found on the <a href="https://scp-int.wikidot.com/">International Translation Archive</a>. If there isn't an official branch yet, you can post translations on the <a href="https://scp-int.wikidot.com/unofficial:language-incubator-hub">Language Incubator</a>. If you have any questions, feel free to ask on the <a href="https://scp-int.wikidot.com/chat-guidelines">SCP International Discord</a>.</p>

<div class="collapsible-block-content">
<p>Any translations posted on the English Language SCP wiki can be linked from this page by adding them to the appropriate section. Feel free to <a href="https://05command.wikidot.com/staff-list">contact staff</a> or ask on the <a href="https://scp-int.wikidot.com/chat-guidelines">INT Discord</a> if you have any questions. You can also post translations on the <a href="https://scp-int.wikidot.com/">International Translation Archive</a>.</p>
<p>These instructions assume you're familiar with our <a href="/translations-policy">Translations Policy</a>, which you should read first, since this focuses on the process of posting a translation.</p>
<p>Translations should be posted by either the author or the translator, or by someone else acting with their permission, and all authors and translators must be credited (either on the discussion page, or in the article using components like the <a href="/component:info-ayers">Info Bar</a>, <a href="/info:start">Info Module</a> or <a href="/component:license-box">License Box</a>). You will also need to message a member of staff to ensure that the article's <a href="/attribution-metadata">Attribution Metadata</a> lists both the author and the translator.</p>
<p>Translations posted to this wiki should have the same unix-name as the original article (for this page it's <tt>scp-international</tt>, the part of the URL after <tt>scp-wiki.wikidot.com/</tt>), although the Title of the page should usually be translated into English. Although it has been done in the past, translations are no longer allowed to be posted as mainlist SCPs. Translations of 001-Proposals from other branches can be posted on the <a href="/scp-001">SCP-001</a> hub, but there's no requirement to do so, since most branches have their own 001 hub for their own proposals.</p>
<p>Translations should be added to the appropriate list below and be tagged with <a href="/system:page-tags/tag/international">"international"</a> and one of the Language Code tags from the Translations tab of the <a href="/tag-list">Tag List</a> (such as <a href="/system:page-tags/tag/_ru">_ru</a> or <a href="/system:page-tags/tag/_cn">_cn</a>), in addition to any other tags that apply. Generally, this will be the same as the tags on the original article, translated into English, but any tags that are not listed on our <a href="/tag-list">Tag List</a> will need to be removed. If posting a translation from a branch that doesn't have a Language Code tag yet, message a member of the <a href="https://05command.wikidot.com/technical-staff-main">Technical Team</a> and they'll add it to the list. If you're unsure about tagging, make sure you apply the <a href="/system:page-tags/tag/international">"international"</a> tag so that people know it's a translation, but leave the rest of the tags to the tag team.</p>
<p>Before posting here, we recommend receiving critique on the translation from someone fluent in English. Minor errors may be overlooked, but poorly worded translations are likely to be downvoted.</p>
