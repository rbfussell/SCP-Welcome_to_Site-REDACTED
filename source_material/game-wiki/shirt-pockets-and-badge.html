Shirt pockets and detachable badge

Implemented November 5: two buttoned shirt chest pockets and a separate badge attached to an exposed clip above the wearer's left pocket. Current reader follow-up: badge-controlled access accepts an exposed worn badge automatically during realtime approach; otherwise equip it in hand and present with E. A bounded reader/credential service now exists; full facility identity/credential administration remains future work. This is game equipment design, not an SCP canon claim. See the current guide and actual evidence.

Physical items and access

The shirt adds chest-left and chest-right, each a provisional 75×100 mm opening/grid, 20 mm deep and 300 g maximum, initially buttoned. Left means wearer-left/viewer-right. Pocket segments link actual contours to normal paid opening, independent fastening menus and scoped storage. Zipped jackets block both physical mouths; opening restores physical access but opaque jacket side panels can still hide small-body targets. The enlarged shirt view remains available.

The clip is an attachment support, not a pocket. personnel-badge is a separate 20 g item, initially inside the spare backpack main compartment. Moving the shirt carries attached badge and pocket contents once each. Independent transfers, ground staging, body inspection and normal time/AP still apply.

Covered retention versus manipulation

Attachment validation adds optional AttachmentProfile.CanBeCovered=false; the badge sets true so an outer jacket may cover it without invalidating the graph. Access traversal honors optional AttachmentSurface.RequiresExposedAccess=false; the clip sets true, requiring its offered contact exposed for source/destination/ancestor access. Covered badges cannot be removed or attached through clothing. Neither flag waives normal interface, mass, collision, ancestry or paid commitment checks. Existing hooks/pouches retain their previous rules.

Domain checks qualify both pockets, limits, coverage, paid badge moves, covered retention/access and exact continuation. Native checks qualify real sprite input/menus, storage, badge clip drops, own badge picking and jacket occlusion. Focused --shirt-pocket-smoke and pocket/full inventory modes run the fixture.

Presentation and identity

The asset library and item catalog expose a new two-pocket burgundy shirt sheet and separate badge sprite, copied from built-in imagegen with unchanged alpha/source bytes. The guide links exact prompts/hashes. The original shirt/jacket atlas remains preserved. Gear attachment metadata optionally names normalized providerOrigin, shared by drawing and picking; default remains centre. Full-body presentation uses a separate attachment plane and actual dressing order. World outfits remain baked.

The native kit now has 20 definitions/40 instances and two extra pocket states. Immutable content identity changes: use a fresh scene for this kit; matching continuation remains schema 15, with no new mutable field. Persistence rejects mismatched old kits atomically.

Door-access boundary

The bounded local reader now checks issuer, clearance, optional assigned actor, condition, exposed wearing/hand placement and authored reader power. Manual presentation costs 500 local ms/2 AP. Production issuance, revocation, expiration, network identity and OSCP account authorization remain future work. Visible wearing is required for automatic entry, not sufficient permission for every door. Covered/stored badges and painted badge pixels never grant automatic authorization. The jacket follow-up removes the painted card from a new art version and supplies a real exterior clip for this same badge. Original source art is preserved. OSCP and facility/world permissions must eventually share authoritative credential services without omniscience.
